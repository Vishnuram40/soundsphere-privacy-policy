Privacy Policy for SoundSphere

Last updated: November 27, 2026 • Application: SoundSphere (Aura Music)

Welcome to SoundSphere ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our local music player and audio workstation application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

SoundSphere is designed as a privacy-focused local offline audio player. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

01. Permissions & Information We Access

To deliver local playback and audio customization features, SoundSphere requires specific device permissions strictly for on-device functionality:

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / Storage Permission

Used solely to scan, index, and playback your locally stored music files (MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, OGG, M4A) on your device. Your audio files are never uploaded to any external server.

RECORD_AUDIO / Visualizer API

Required strictly by the Android Visualizer engine to capture live audio frequency bands in real-time and render the 16-bar waveform animations. We do not record, save, or transmit your voice or ambient microphone audio.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS / Foreground Service

Required to display persistent media playback controls in your notification tray and lock screen (Play/Pause, Track Seek, Mood Effects Switcher) while the app runs smoothly in the background.

WRITE_SETTINGS (Ringtone Maker)

Used only when you explicitly choose to set a trimmed audio track as your phone ringtone, notification, or alarm tone.

02. Optional AI Features and User-Provided API Keys (BYOK Policy)

SoundSphere offers optional artificial intelligence capabilities, including AI Mood Playlist Generation and natural-language library discovery. We maintain an uncompromising approach to privacy, user autonomy, and data minimization:

Disabled by Default & Strictly Opt-In

All AI-powered features are disabled by default upon installation. SoundSphere makes zero outbound AI network connections, queries, or telemetry calls unless and until a user explicitly chooses to enable AI capabilities within the app settings.

User-Provided API Keys (No Bundled or Shared Keys)

SoundSphere does not provide, embed, distribute, or bundle its own Gemini API key or any proprietary AI credentials. SoundSphere operates strictly under a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) model. To activate optional AI capabilities, users must voluntarily obtain and connect their own personal third-party API key (such as a Google AI Studio Gemini API key) through the app settings.

Local On-Device Key Security & Storage

When a user configures an API key, it is stored strictly and locally on the user's physical device using Android's encrypted private storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences). SoundSphere developers never see, collect, log, or transmit your API key to any intermediary server. Users can delete, clear, or modify their API key at any time directly in Settings.

Data Transmission During AI Processing

When a user actively invokes an AI feature (for example, generating a playlist by submitting a prompt such as "Workout rush with heavy bass" or "Rainy afternoon study session"):

  • The specific text prompt and minimal relevant genre/tempo parameters are transmitted directly from your device to the selected third-party AI provider (e.g., Google Gemini API) via secure HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII), names, email addresses, device identifiers (IMEI/AAID), contact lists, location data, or audio files are ever included or transmitted in the AI request.
  • Requests are routed directly to the third-party provider's official API endpoints without routing through any developer-owned proxy servers.

Third-Party Provider Terms & User Responsibility

Users are solely responsible for obtaining, managing, and maintaining the security of their own API keys. Use of third-party AI services is governed by the respective provider's terms of service and privacy policies (e.g., Google Privacy Policy and Google AI Terms of Service). Users are responsible for complying with the provider's acceptable use guidelines, token quotas, and rate limits.

100% Offline Core Music Player Guarantee

Users can choose not to connect an API key and enjoy SoundSphere as a completely offline local music player without missing any audio capabilities. All core features — including lossless audio playback (FLAC, MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG, M4A), the 10-Band Pro Studio Equalizer, real-time audio visualizers, playlist management, metadata tag editing, and sleep timer — remain 100% functional with zero internet access, zero account creation, and zero API keys.

03. Local Storage & Room Database

All playback statistics (Total Listening Hours, Most Played Hits, Aura Insights), custom playlists, favorite songs, and 10-Band equalizer presets are stored strictly on your device's local Room SQLite Database.

When you use the Database Backup & Restore tool, the exported JSON file is stored locally in your chosen directory or shared only via your device's native Android share sheet.

04. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertisements

While the current version of SoundSphere focuses on local offline playback, we may integrate third-party services in upcoming versions to maintain application performance, diagnose crashes, and support ongoing development:

Google Play Services

Used for basic app infrastructure, licensing checks, and automatic in-app updates.

Third-Party Advertising (e.g., Google AdMob)

Future updates may display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob or certified ad partners. These partners may collect pseudonymous identifiers such as your device's Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and interaction data. You can reset or opt out anytime via Android Settings (Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads).

Analytics & Performance Monitoring (e.g., Firebase)

We may utilize diagnostic SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics) to collect aggregated, anonymous crash logs and load times to optimize audio engine performance across Android devices.

All third-party partners adhere to strict data protection standards (e.g., Google Privacy & Terms).

05. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SoundSphere does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will promptly remove it.

06. Security of Your Data

Because your music files and listening statistics remain locally on your physical device, your security is protected by your device security and standard Android sandbox protections.

07. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
